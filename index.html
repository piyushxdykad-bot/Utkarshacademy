Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Utkarsh Academy collects, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe when you use our website and courses.

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

1 Information We Collect

When you register for a course, attempt a mock test, or contact us through the website, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details — your name, phone number, email address, city and school/college name.
  • Academic details — the class (11, 12, CUET), subjects and batch you enrol in.
  • Account information — your login email and encrypted password, managed securely through our authentication provider.
  • Test & performance data — your mock test scores, attempted questions, submitted answer copies, and study progress.
  • Communication data — messages you send us via the contact form or WhatsApp.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process your enrolment, set up your student account, and give you access to the correct course content.
  • Evaluate your mock tests and show you your results, rank and performance trends.
  • Send you important updates about batches, class schedules, results and announcements.
  • Respond to your queries and provide academic or admission support.
  • Improve our courses, website and teaching material based on aggregate usage patterns.

3 Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and local storage to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can disable cookies from your browser settings, though some features such as staying logged in may not work correctly if you do.

4 Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Your information may only be shared with:

  • Trusted service providers who help us run the website, host our database and send communications (for example, our cloud database and email/WhatsApp providers), solely to deliver those services.
  • Government or regulatory authorities, where required by law.

5 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure, encrypted cloud infrastructure with access-controlled databases. While we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6 Your Rights & Choices

You can request to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the details below. You may also opt out of promotional messages while continuing to receive essential academic communication about your enrolled course.

7 Children's Privacy

Many of our students are minors. Where a student is below 18 years of age, we expect a parent or guardian to review this policy and supervise the enrolment process. We only collect information necessary to provide our educational services.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

We recommend checking this page periodically for the latest version.

9 Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us:

  • Email: info@utkarshacademy.com
  • Phone: +91 98765 43210
